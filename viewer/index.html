Reading the numbers

On surface (precision) and coverage (recall)

On surface: share of a run's points within the threshold of the ground-truth surface. Coverage: share of ground-truth points with a run point within the threshold.

Thresholds and the ceiling

Distances are in the object's unit sphere. The ground truth has 4096 points, about 1.6% of the radius apart, so even a perfect surface scores only about 0.28 at 1%. The ceiling row shows that limit per object; 2% and 5% are more informative.

Comparing runs

Runs can cover different objects. Category means only use each run's own objects, so compare runs on the objects they share (the list shows every run's score per object).
