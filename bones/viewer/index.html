Reading the numbers

On surface (precision)

Share of COLMAP points within the threshold of the ground-truth surface. High means the points that exist are correct.

Coverage (recall)

Share of ground-truth points with a COLMAP point within the threshold. Sparse SIFT points only land on textured areas, so plain and shiny objects stay low.

Thresholds and the ceiling

Distances are measured with the object scaled into a unit sphere. The ground truth has 4096 points, about 1.6% of the radius apart, so even a perfect surface scores only about 0.28 at 1%. The 2% and 5% columns say more about sparse output.

True cameras vs blind SfM

True cameras: COLMAP gets the 24 known poses and only triangulates. Blind: COLMAP estimates the poses itself and often links only part of the 24 views; the result is aligned to the true cameras before scoring.

Ground-truth alignment

The ground-truth cloud is fitted to each object's 24 silhouettes. Objects where under 90% of its points fall inside the silhouettes are shown but not scored.
